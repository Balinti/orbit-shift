How to Play Orbit Shift

Your ship auto-orbits around the galactic core. Tap (or press Space/Enter) near a Bridge to hop to the adjacent ring. Dodge debris, collect crystal shards, and survive as long as possible!

Tips & Tricks

  • Tap right as your ship enters a bridge window for a Perfect Hop bonus.
  • Inner rings spawn more Crystal Shards — worth 5 points each.
  • Near-miss a debris block without dying for a Graze Bonus.
  • After 70 seconds, electrified red ring segments appear — hop away quickly!
  • Chaining perfect hops builds a combo multiplier for big scores.

FAQ

Q: Is Orbit Shift free? A: Yes, completely free to play in any browser.

Q: Does it work on mobile? A: Yes — tap anywhere to hop between rings.

Q: How is the high score saved? A: Stored locally in your browser via localStorage.
