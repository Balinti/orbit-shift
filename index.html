How to Play Orbit Shift

Orbit Shift is a free hyper-casual HTML5 browser game. Your satellite constantly orbits a planet at a fixed radius. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter to instantly flip 180 degrees to the opposite side of the orbit. Incoming asteroids travel in straight lines toward the planet center — dodge them by flipping at the right moment. Earn near-miss bonuses for close calls, build a combo multiplier, and see how long you can survive. Difficulty increases over time with faster asteroids, double-strike waves, and feint warning lines that appear to trick you. A shield protects you for the first 3 seconds. Your best score is automatically saved. Fully playable on mobile and desktop.
